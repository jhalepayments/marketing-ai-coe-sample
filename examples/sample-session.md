# Sample session

A contractor from an outside agency uses `/campaign-launch-coach` for the first time. The campaign, people, and dates are invented (illustrative). The coach stops once, logs a gap, and carries on after a human answers.

---

**Contractor:** /campaign-launch-coach

**Coach:** Hi. I'll walk you through launching one email campaign for Jhale Payments, one step at a time. If anything is unclear, I'll stop and tell you who to ask rather than guess.

Step 1, brief intake. Please give me the goal, audience, offer, send date, and owner.

**Contractor:** Goal: get small business customers to turn on Jhale Payouts. Audience: small business customers who joined this year. Offer: no setup fee until January 31. Send date: December 9, 10am Eastern. Owner: the Lifecycle marketing manager.

**Coach:** That's complete. Every field is specific, so we can build from it.

Step 2, audience and compliance. Has everyone on the list opted in to marketing email? And which regions does the list cover?

**Contractor:** Yes, everyone opted in. The list is the US, the UK, and some customers in Ireland. Ireland is close to the UK, can I use the UK footer?

**Coach:** I'm going to stop here. This guide has footer rules for the US, the UK, and Canada, but not Ireland, and a wrong footer is a legal problem, not a style one. Please ask the **Compliance partner** which footer Ireland needs.

I've added this to the gap log:

| Date | Step | What was unclear | What the person asked or said | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-12-01 | 2. Audience and compliance | List includes Ireland. Guide has no footer rule for Ireland. | "Ireland is close to the UK, can I use the UK footer?" | open |

Come back when you have an answer.

**Contractor:** The Compliance partner says to use the UK footer, but swap in the Irish regulator line. They sent the text.

**Coach:** Thanks, that unblocks us. The gap stays open until the monthly review, when Ireland gets added to the guide properly.

Step 3, content check. Please paste the draft.

*(Steps 3 to 5 pass with no stops. The draft passes all six brand rules, all seven QA items are checked, and the Lifecycle marketing manager approves in writing.)*

**Coach:**

```
Campaign: Payouts for new small businesses
Goal: small business customers turn on Jhale Payouts
Audience and region: small businesses who joined this year; US, UK, Ireland
Send date: December 9, 10am Eastern
Approved by: Lifecycle marketing manager
Watching after send: Lifecycle marketing manager
Gaps logged this session: 1 (Ireland footer)
```

Thanks. The Ireland gap will be reviewed this month and added to the guide, so the next person won't get stuck there.
