# Monthly gap review

**What it is:** a 30-minute meeting (illustrative) once a month to turn open gaps into skill updates.
**Who:** the skill owner (Marketing Ops lead) plus whichever roles the open gaps point to.
**Input:** every row in `gap-log.md` with status "open."

## The steps

1. **Sort.** Group the open gaps by step. Gaps that repeat go first, because they affect the most people.
2. **Decide.** For each gap, pick one:
   - **Update the skill:** the answer is the same every time, so write it into the guide. Example: add Europe's footer to Step 2.
   - **Name an owner:** the answer depends on judgment, so the skill should keep stopping, but it needs to name the right person. Example: list a backup approver in Step 5.
   - **Leave it:** a one-off that won't come up again. Close it with a short note.
3. **Get the answer.** The role the gap points to gives the real answer. The skill owner never makes it up.
4. **Edit the skill.** Make the change in `SKILL.md`. Keep it short.
5. **Close the row.** Change the status to "closed: " plus what changed, so anyone can trace why the skill says what it says.
6. **Tell people.** Post one line to the teams that use the skill: what changed and why.

## How we know it's working

If the skill is learning, fewer people should get stuck each month. Gaps logged by month (illustrative):

| Month | Gaps logged |
|---|---|
| August | 14 |
| September | 9 |
| October | 6 |
| November | 3 |

The rows in `gap-log.md` are a sample of these, not the full set. A rising count is also useful: it usually means a new region, product, or team started using the skill.
