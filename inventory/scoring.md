# Scoring ideas

Each idea gets two scores, from 1 to 3.

**Feasibility: how easy is it to build?**
- 3: a checklist or guide we already have, turned into a skill
- 2: needs some new content or a decision from another team
- 1: needs new data, a system connection, or specialist skills

**Reach: how many people does it help?**
- 3: several teams, often
- 2: one team, often, or several teams, rarely
- 1: a few people, rarely

**Total = feasibility + reach (2 to 6).** Higher totals get built first. An idea that's easy to build and broadly useful always goes to the top.

## Worked example (illustrative)

| Idea | Feasibility | Reach | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| results-summary | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| localization-check | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| webinar-follow-up | 2 | 1 | 3 |

## Who builds it

- **Build in-house** when feasibility is 2 or 3. The team that proposed it builds it with the Marketing Ops lead, using an existing skill as a starting point.
- **Send to an agency** when feasibility is 1, or the in-house team has no room this quarter. The agency gets the intake form, a written brief, and an existing skill to copy so the result matches ours.
- **Either way,** the finished skill comes back into `INVENTORY.md` with a named owner, follows the stop-and-ask rule, and joins the monthly gap review. Nothing is "live" without an owner.
