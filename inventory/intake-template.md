# Propose a new skill

Anyone on any team can propose a skill. Copy this form, fill it in, and send it to the Marketing Ops lead. It takes about ten minutes (illustrative). Check `INVENTORY.md` first in case something close already exists.

---

**Name for the idea:**

**1. What's the problem?**
What goes wrong or takes too long today? One or two sentences.

**2. Who does it help?**
Which teams or roles, and roughly how many people.

**3. How often does it come up?**
Daily, weekly, per campaign, a few times a year?

**4. What does it replace?**
What do people do today instead: a checklist, a spreadsheet, asking a specific person, or nothing?

**Anything close in the inventory already?**
If yes, why doesn't adapting it work?

---

**What happens next:** the Marketing Ops lead scores it using `scoring.md`, adds it to `INVENTORY.md` as "proposed," and tells you whether it will be built in-house or sent to an agency.
