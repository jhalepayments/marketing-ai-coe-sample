# AI inventory

Every skill at Jhale Payments, built or proposed, in one place. Before you build something, check here. Someone may have already built it, and adapting it is faster than starting over.

Anyone can add a row. See `how-to-adapt.md` to adapt a skill and `intake-template.md` to propose a new one.

| Name | What it does | Owner | Status | Who can use it | How to adapt it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| campaign-launch-coach | Walks an outside campaign manager through launching an email campaign in five steps. Stops and asks a human when unsure. | Marketing Ops lead | live | Marketing team and contracted agencies | Swap the brand guide, region rules, QA list, and the "who to ask" table for your team's |
| brief-qa | Checks a campaign brief for missing or unclear fields before work starts | Marketing Ops lead | live | Anyone who writes or receives a campaign brief | Change the field table to match your team's brief template |
| webinar-follow-up | Drafts follow-up emails for webinar attendees and no-shows from the session notes | Events lead | proposed | Events and product marketing | Not built yet |
| localization-check | Checks translated emails for the right footer, currency, and date format per region | Compliance partner | proposed | Any team sending outside the US | Not built yet |
| results-summary | Turns a campaign's results into a one-paragraph summary for the weekly update | Marketing analytics lead | proposed | All marketing teams | Not built yet |

**Status meanings:** "proposed" means submitted and scored but not built yet. "live" means built, tested, and has an owner who reviews its gaps monthly. "retired" means no longer kept up to date, so don't use it.
